- 🟢 Toasts stack over the task cards while jammed (found in phase 09): "Systems jammed. The task was reset." sits on the Start button for a few seconds (same toast-placement polish as earlier phases, Phase 13).
- 🟢 A crew player who reaches a fake's spot first reveals it like any task (found in phase 09): that is the point, but a Phantom cannot tell when a crew member is on their fake except through the dots.
- 🟠 Meetings untested with real people (found in phase 08): the 180 s gather, the 30 m Base radius, the stage timings and the screens are proven with the simulator, bots and sockets only. Tune `meetingGatherS`/`baseRadiusM` after a field test (Manual actions 00000).
- 🟢 A meeting's imposter-vs-crew behaviour is untuned (found in phase 08): bots vote at random, so a bot game rarely ejects the right player. Phase 11's balance script should give bots a suspicion model if it wants meaningful meeting statistics.
- 🟢 The Infect button shows "recharging" for 30 s after a meeting, not "after the meeting" (found in phase 08, ADR-073).
- 🟢 `postMeetingGraceS` applies to Infect only (found in phase 08): Phase 9 must add it to the new abilities.
- 🟢 Meetings do not advance section-entry holds (found in phase 08): holds run on game time, which is frozen, so the MEETINGS §3 note that reveals keep progressing holds only for the position, not the timer. A player who was 2 s into a hold finishes it after the meeting.
- 🟢 The vote has no per-voter timeout nudge (found in phase 08): a player who never votes simply counts as Skip when the 45 s end.
- 🟠 Infection untested at real distances (found in phase 07): 8 m and 10 s are proven with the simulator and teleports only. Real GPS error (often 5 to 15 m) may make the channel break (3 s gap tolerance) or make "in range" flicker; tune `infectionRadiusM` and `infectionGapToleranceS` after a field test (Manual actions 0000).
- 🟢 Gap tolerance is per gap (found in phase 07, RULES §7.1 clarification): an imposter who alternates 1 s in range and 3 s out could finish a channel with less than half the time in range. If playtests show it, cap the total gap time per channel.
- 🟢 Infect button can lag the radar (found in phase 07, ADR-071): a frame may be up to `radarIntervalMs` old, so "No one in range" can show for about a second after a target arrives. The server decides.
- 🟢 Toasts cover the imposter bar (found in phase 07): "Someone has been compromised" and "Exposed…" stack over the Infect panel for a few seconds (same toast-placement polish as Phase 5, Phase 13).
- 🟢 Exposed and infected dots only show for players on the radar (found in phase 07): in `regions` mode the radar is empty by design, so the imposter has the list (`Infected: n`) but no dots.
- 🟢 Bots: in-process chase bots read positions from the room state, the CLI bots from the radar (found in phase 07): the CLI bots need visibility `Everyone` to hunt anything beyond `nearbyRadiusM`.
- 🟢 Render free tier sleeps and restarts end lobbies (found in phase 06): warm the URL before a test or demo; avoid pushing to `main` during a playtest (every push redeploys).
- 🟠 Tasks untested on real phones (found in phase 05): the section hold, the 15 m reach, hysteresis and the cues are proven with the simulator and teleports only. Real GPS drift near a section edge or a pin could make the hold or "at the pin" flicker; tune `sectionEntryHoldS`/`reachRadiusM` after the Phase 6 field test (Manual actions 00).
- 🟢 Initial JS is 152 KB gzipped (found in phase 05; was 133 KB): the minigame types, `pointInSection` and `sectionShapes` joined the shared client bundle. Budget is 300 KB (ARCHITECTURE §11). The games themselves are lazy chunks (1–2 KB each).
- 🟢 Task toasts overlap the task cards (found in phase 05): "Pin revealed" and "You are at the pin" stack over the panel for a few seconds. Phase 13 polish: move toasts or drop the duplicates now that the card changes.
- 🟢 Wires is tap-to-pair, not drag (found in phase 05, ADR-063): TASKS §4 said drag; taps suit one hand and screen readers. Revisit in Phase 13 if playtesters want drag.
- 🟢 Bots work only their first task card (found in phase 05): they never reroll or report, and imposter bots do nothing yet (Phase 7+).
- 🟢 Sections bigger than a few hundred metres across mean long walks (found in phase 05): at the 3 s hold the player must stay in the section; a section the player is walking through reveals the pin only if they pause. That matches the spec; watch it in the field test.
- 🟢 Task points are not re-checked against no-go zones after a game starts (found in phase 05): the host can't edit setup during play, so pool points are always valid.
- 🟠 Role-hold button untested on real phones (found in phase 04): pointer capture and the hold gesture are proven in Chromium only. Check iOS Safari (long-press may select text or open a callout; the button sets `touch-none`, `select-none` and blocks the context menu) and Android Chrome (Manual actions 0).
- 🟢 Spawn failure is only discovered when the countdown ends (found in phase 04, ADR-052): a lobby whose area can't fit the spawns passes the checklist, then everyone is un-readied with a toast. Rare (a valid setup holds ≥ 4 points per player).
- 🟢 Secret seed is 32 bits (found in phase 04, ADR-050): derived from a random 24-symbol salt. Enough for this game's threat model; widen the RNG state if the stakes change.
- 🟢 Time-up and forfeit resolve on the 1 Hz tick or the next command, so a game can end up to about 1 s late (found in phase 04).
- 🟢 Bots v1 only walk (found in phase 04): they don't do tasks, infect or vote yet. Phase 5, 7 and 8 extend `BotDriver` and `tools/bots`.
- 🟢 In-process bots count as connected only while a human is connected (found in phase 04, ADR-056): if every human drops, the bots go offline too, so an abandoned demo room is swept after 30 minutes.
- 🟢 `game.meetingsLeft` echoes `meetingsPerPlayer` until meetings exist (found in phase 04).
- 🟢 Role cards can't be re-read after the reveal except in the HUD chip (found in phase 04): the HUD shows role, tag and allies in plain text. A hold-to-reveal on the HUD is a Phase 13 polish candidate; today it is visible to a glance, as UI_UX §2 accepts for the red/blue themes.
- 🟢 Setup payload (found in phase 03): the whole setup (regions, points, zones, Base) is about 8–25 KB and is re-sent inside every player's view on any change (ready toggles, fixes). Fine for 12 players; if bandwidth matters, send the map once and diff it (Phase 13/14).
- 🟢 Tapping under your own marker (found in phase 03): a task point exactly beneath the host's own position marker can't be tapped to delete it. Move or zoom the map.
- 🟢 Section polygons vs `sectionOf` (found in phase 03, ADR-044): the preview polygons use a flat-plane bearing, `sectionOf` the geodesic one, so they can disagree by up to about 1.5° at a wedge border. Only the display is affected.
- 🟢 Isolated region islands (found in phase 03, ADR-049): a no-go zone can leave a region in several parts, or an island with no neighbour; the compass still works, the label sits at the one origin.
- 🟢 Lobby radar is `nearby` by default (found in phase 03, ADR-047): players gathering far apart don't see each other in the lobby unless the host picks "Everyone".
- 🟢 Kick is per session, not per person (found in phase 01): `lobby:join` carries no token, so a kicked player can rejoin under a new name from a fresh session. The host can kick again. Fixing it properly needs accounts (out of scope, ADR-029/037).
- 🟢 Countdown resolution (found in phase 01): the countdown expires on the 1 Hz tick, so `starting` can arrive up to `tickIntervalMs` late. Fine for a placeholder; Phase 4 could schedule an exact timer.
- 🟢 Initial JS is 128 KB gzipped (budget 300 KB, ARCHITECTURE §11); Leaflet, Geoman and the setup layers are a separate lazy 139 KB chunk loaded when a lobby opens. zod and turf are in the shared client bundle.
- 🟠 Real-phone GPS untested (found in phase 02): the simulator and server rules are tested, but accuracy, wake lock and the permission flow on iOS Safari and Android Chrome need the optional phone check (Manual actions).
- 🟢 OSM tiles (found in phase 02, ADR-039): fine for a small event; OSM's tile policy doesn't suit a public launch. Revisit a keyed provider in Phase 14.
- 🟢 A weak, noisy fix can make the next good fix look like a jump and delay re-anchoring by up to 3 fixes (ADR-040).
- 🟢 Boundary vertices (found in phase 02): a circle becomes a 64-vertex polygon, so editing its corners in Geoman is fiddly. Redrawing is easier.
- 🟢 Local port clash (found in phase 00): another project's Vite server listens on `[::1]:5173`, so `http://localhost:5173` can open that app instead. Use `http://127.0.0.1:5173`. Playwright already does.
- 🟢 Status-dot colours (found in phase 00): the connected/connecting dot uses Tailwind `emerald`/`amber` rather than theme tokens, because UI_UX §1 has no success or warning token. Revisit when the design system grows (Phase 13).
