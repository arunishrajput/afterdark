One of you is lying.

AfterDark is Among Us played on your feet: a real-world, GPS social-deduction game that runs in your phone's browser. No app store. No accounts. Share a 6-letter code and play.

🔵 CREW follow clues to hidden task spots around your city and solve quick puzzles.
🔴 IMPOSTERS secretly infect players just by staying close, jam every task, plant fake tasks, or disguise their screen as crew.
🚨 ANYONE can call an emergency meeting. Everyone walks to the Base, argues face to face, and votes someone out.

▶ PLAY FREE: https://afterdark-24gx.onrender.com
(The free server sleeps when idle, so the first load can take up to a minute.)

THE GAME
• 4–12 players, in any open, safe place you choose
• The host draws the play area on a real map
• 7 minigames, 3 imposter classes (Saboteur, Phantom, Camouflage)
• Secret roles, secret infections, no in-app chat. You talk in person.
• Safety first: the app never tells you to run. It's "walk, never run", with a "Stop somewhere safe" check before every puzzle.

UNDER THE HOOD
Server-authoritative game engine, per-player views so a crew phone never receives hidden info, 1,100+ automated tests. Installable PWA.

SOURCE CODE: https://github.com/arunishrajput/afterdark

Built for the Code N Mode hackathon at SIT, organised by 32 BIT. Trailer made with Remotion; the score is original and synthesized in code.
